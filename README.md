## About Me

I am a **Computer Science graduate from BRAC University, Bangladesh**, with a research focus on **deep learning and computer vision for remote sensing, GeoAI, and environmental monitoring**.

My research interest lies in using **satellite imagery and AI to monitor and understand environmental phenomena**, particularly where conventional monitoring is limited by data availability, cloud cover, or complex environmental conditions.

My primary research interests include:

* 🛰️ **Remote Sensing & GeoAI**
* 🌍 **Environmental Monitoring**
* 👁️ **Computer Vision**
* 🧠 **Deep Learning & Self-Supervised Learning**
* 📡 **SAR & Multispectral Imagery**
* 🌊 **Riverbank Erosion & Change Detection**
* 🌱 **Agricultural & Environmental Applications of AI**

## Research

My undergraduate research focused on **riverbank erosion detection and monitoring** using satellite imagery.

I worked on **Jamuna Riverbank Change Detection** using Sentinel-2 multispectral imagery and machine learning methods, published at **IEEE ACDSA 2025**.

My undergraduate thesis addressed the limitations of optical satellite imagery, particularly cloud coverage, by developing a **self-supervised framework combining Masked Autoencoders and Mamba for semantic segmentation of Sentinel-1 SAR imagery** for riverbank erosion detection.

Beyond remote sensing, I have also worked on computer vision and generative AI:

* **MFSAA — IEEE Eurocon 2025:** Multi-scale feature fusion and adaptive attention for robust image deblurring.
* **TWIG — Springer IFIP 2025:** Segmentation-guided diffusion for controlled image generation.
* **Riverbank Change Detection — IEEE ACDSA 2025:** Satellite-image-based monitoring of riverbank changes.
* **Remote Sensing & Environmental Monitoring:** Deep learning approaches for satellite imagery and environmental applications.
